## Overview

Web platform for **Mesoskin Hamburg** — an aesthetic medicine and skin health practice offering infusion therapy, PDO thread lifts, hyaluronic acid treatments and mesotherapy, alongside **Glamour Akademie**, its continuing-education arm for practitioners.

Two audiences, one site: a prospective client deciding whether to trust someone with their face, and a working practitioner deciding whether to book a masterclass.

### Key features

- **Treatment catalogue** — Dripspas infusion therapy, PDO threads, hyaluronic acid, mesotherapy and lip work, each explained in plain language rather than clinical shorthand
- **Glamour Akademie section** — the training arm presented as its own destination: single modules, masterclasses, and 1:1 mentoring in the practitioner's own clinic
- **Consultation path** — a clear route from browsing a treatment to requesting a personal consultation
- **Editorial gallery** — masonry image layout for before/after and clinic photography
- **Video integration** — embedded treatment and practice footage via react-player
- **Motion design** — Framer Motion reveals and Lenis smooth scroll, tuned to feel calm and clinical rather than flashy
- **Animated statistics** — count-up figures for experience and treatment volume
- **Performance-optimised imagery** — sharp-backed Next.js image pipeline for a photo-heavy site

## Built for

**Mesoskin Hamburg** — an aesthetic medicine practice in Hamburg, with **Annette Fascher-Wendlandt** as aesthetic lead. The practice works from a Heilpraktikerin and psychological-counselling background, with an explicitly integrative philosophy: body, mind and spirit treated as one system, and treatment plans built per client rather than off a menu.

### The brief

Aesthetic medicine is a trust purchase. Someone is choosing a person to inject their face — the website is often the only evidence they have before they commit. The site needed to:

- Read as a medical practice with genuine expertise, not a beauty salon with a price list
- Explain technical treatments — PDO threads, mesotherapy, infusion therapy — to people encountering the terms for the first time
- Carry the practitioner's integrative philosophy as substance, not as a decorative quote
- Give Glamour Akademie enough room to function as its own offering, addressing Heilpraktikerinnen, Ärztinnen and ambitious professionals rather than end clients
- Handle a heavy photographic load — the work is visual, and the proof is in the images — without a slow, heavy page
- Convert quietly, through consultation requests rather than hard-sell funnels
